Disciplined AI Judgment: Knowing When NOT to Use AI

Interviewers love to test candidates on whether they understand the limits of LLMs. Here is the exact architectural boundary:

🚫 Where We DO NOT Use AI:
  • • Ledger & Tax Arithmetic: GST (18%), discounts, and burn rates use pure Python floating-point math to eliminate hallucinations.
  • • Security & RBAC Ceilings: ₹50K HITL threshold and token limits are validated deterministically in Pydantic models.
  • • Audit Chaining: SHA-256 cryptographic hashing links compliance records immutably.
🤖 Where We DO Use AI:
  • • Natural Language Understanding: Extracting semantic purchase intent from conversational requests.
  • • Causal Root Cause Correlation: Linking operational signals with spending anomalies.
  • • Multi-Agent Role-Aware Narration: Tailoring executive explanations for CFOs vs Auditors.

Top 10 Technical Interview Questions & Answers

Q1: How do you prevent AI hallucinations in financial calculations?

Answer: We enforce strict architectural boundaries. All arithmetic (18% GST, burn rate calculations, subtotal sums, and budget balances) is computed deterministically in Python. The LLM is only used for intent parsing, causal correlation, and semantic narrative formulation.

Q2: How does the AI-to-AI Shopping protocol work?

Answer: We expose a machine-readable JSON-LD catalog manifest at `/v1/commerce/ai-manifest`. External buyer agents send purchase requests with an authorized `X-Spending-Token-Limit` header. Our merchant agent validates the ceiling, applies bulk discount rules within policy constraints (max 15%), and returns a test-mode Razorpay payment link.

Q3: How does the Financial Digital Twin simulate 90 days forward?

Answer: It takes an in-memory snapshot of liquid reserves, department budgets, and scheduled invoices, then applies day-by-day differential equations with modifier multipliers (burn spikes, payment delays, headcount changes) to project daily cash continuity and runway days.

Q4: How do you enforce strict role-based notification isolation?

Answer: Notifications are filtered at the service query layer against `recipientUserId == current_user.id` or explicit `observerIds`. Senders, recipients, and observers are isolated so no department head or auditor can see private CFO requests.

Q5: What happens when Razorpay API drops or times out?

Answer: Our 4-Tier Failure Resilience Engine kicks in with an exponential backoff retry mechanism (3 attempts with jitter) using idempotency keys. If the gateway remains unresponsive, the transaction is queued in an offline state and an escalation ticket is dispatched.

Q6: What is the tech stack and how is it tested?

Answer: Backend: Python 3.11, FastAPI, Pydantic, LangChain/LangGraph, SQLite/In-memory store. Frontend: Tailwind CSS, Vanilla JS, Lucide icons, Chart.js. We have 7 automated test suites with 100% pass rate including Chrome CDP browser automation tests.
